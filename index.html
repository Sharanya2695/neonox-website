ABOUT NEONOX

Engineering the infrastructure surrounding gas use.

NEONOX GAS TECH is a Bengaluru-based engineering and technical services company specializing in high-purity gas pipeline systems, gas regulators and control systems, gas manifold systems, SS tubing installations, precision orbital welding, fabrication, installation, testing and maintenance.

We provide the gas infrastructure and associated engineering solutions rather than the supply of gases, supporting complete or partial systems for critical applications.
